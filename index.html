The Full Stack, In-House

Who We Are

We make industrial plants run themselves — from the panel to the historian to the boardroom dashboard.

Modern plants generate enormous complexity: thousands of I/O points, dozens of control loops, and a constant demand for production data. Most integrators stop at the controller. We don't.

Apex delivers the full stack — from switchboard and control panel fabrication, through PLC and SCADA development, up to custom MES software that turns raw process data into reporting your operations and management teams actually use.

Phase 01

Define

We start on site, not in a sales deck. Process walk-downs, I/O audits, and functional specifications that capture how your plant actually runs — and where it's losing time and money.

Phase 02

Engineer

Panels are built and tested in-house. Code is written against the spec, simulated, and factory acceptance tested before it ever touches your plant. No surprises at cutover.

Phase 03

Commission & Support

Staged commissioning planned around your production schedule, operator training at handover, and ongoing support after go-live. We stay accountable for what we build.
