Emergency Lighting Inspection

Emergency and exit lighting testing, maintenance and six-monthly inspections to AS/NZS 2293 — ensuring your emergency lighting will perform when it matters most.

Get a Free Quote
01
Why Emergency Lighting Must Be Tested
In a power failure or emergency evacuation, emergency lighting is critical for safe egress. AS/NZS 2293 mandates regular testing to confirm that luminaires, batteries and central battery systems will perform during an emergency. Failure to test exposes occupants to risk and your business to legal liability.
02
Monthly Functional Tests
AS/NZS 2293 requires a monthly functional test of every emergency luminaire — confirming the lamp operates on battery power for a brief period. We can provide a documented monthly testing program carried out by our qualified technicians.
03
Six-Monthly Full Duration Tests
Every six months, all emergency luminaires must be discharged for their full rated duration (90 minutes for most systems) and the illuminance levels verified. We carry this out after-hours to avoid operational disruption and document every result.
04
Annual Inspection
The annual inspection covers the full system — luminaires, wiring, central battery systems, test facilities and documentation. We produce a comprehensive inspection report noting defects and remediation requirements.
05
Battery Replacement
Emergency lighting batteries degrade over time and must be replaced before capacity falls below the required level. We test battery condition during inspections and recommend replacement before failure occurs in service.
06
New System Design & Installation
We design and install emergency lighting systems for new buildings and fit-outs, ensuring coverage meets AS/NZS 2293 requirements. We produce the required commissioning documentation for the certifier and building owner.
