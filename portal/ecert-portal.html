What this builds

A structured CCEW lodgement worksheet from your plain-English description — capturing customer, site, work performed and testing — ready to enter into the official portal. It does not issue or replace the official certificate.

02

You stay in control

Nothing is final until you approve. Every field is editable, and the certificate is marked DRAFT until you sign off as the licensed person responsible for the work.

03

Lodging officially

After approval, print to PDF or use "Copy for NSW portal" to paste the details into the official NSW eCert Portal ↗ for lodgement.

IMPORTANT — This tool produces a draft only to speed up your paperwork. It does not lodge anything with NSW Fair Trading and is not a substitute for the official eCert Portal. The licensed electrical worker / qualified supervisor named on the certificate is solely responsible for verifying every detail, confirming the work complies with AS/NZS 3000 and all relevant standards, and lodging the official certificate. Always check the generated content carefully before approving.
