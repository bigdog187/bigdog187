Industrial Installations & Wiring

From greenfield factory fit-outs to adding capacity to existing plant, Weiley Electrical handles the full scope of industrial electrical installations across Central West NSW.

Get a Free Quote
01
New Factory Fit-Outs
We design and install complete electrical systems for new industrial facilities — from service entry and main switchboards through to machinery connections, lighting and small power. Our team manages the project from inception through final certification.
02
Machinery Wiring & Connections
Connecting new equipment or relocating plant? We wire motors, drives, control panels and ancillary equipment to manufacturer specifications and Australian standards, ensuring safe commissioning and correct overload protection.
03
Distribution Board Upgrades
Overloaded boards are a safety and reliability risk. We upgrade main and sub-distribution boards to accommodate increased loads, adding circuit protection, metering and fault monitoring as required.
04
Cable Management & Containment
Industrial sites demand robust cable management. We install cable trays, conduit, ducts and underground pits to protect cabling from physical damage, moisture and chemical exposure.
05
Power Quality & Metering
Poor power quality costs money and damages equipment. We install power factor correction, harmonic filters and sub-metering to give you visibility and control over your energy consumption.
06
Project Management
We coordinate with builders, OEMs and other trades to keep your project on schedule. Our ISO 9001 quality system means documentation, inspections and handover are handled correctly every time.
