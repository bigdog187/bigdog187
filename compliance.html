Service Modules

What we deliver.

Each service is delivered by licensed electricians under our ISO 9001 quality and ISO 45001 safety management systems.

COM-01

Compliance Auditing

Full site electrical audits with detailed written reports for insurance, regulatory and due-diligence purposes.

  • Site audits
  • Defect registers
  • Rectification plans
  • Written reports
COM-02

RCD Testing

Residual current device trip-time and push-button testing with certification and records.

  • Trip-time testing
  • Push-button tests
  • Certification
  • Records
COM-03

Test & Tag

Portable appliance inspection, testing and tagging to AS/NZS 3760: on-site, efficient and documented.

  • Portable appliances
  • Leads & boards
  • Asset registers
  • Re-test reminders
COM-04

Emergency & Exit Lighting

Six-monthly discharge testing, maintenance and logbooks for emergency and exit lighting to AS/NZS 2293.

  • 6-monthly testing
  • Logbooks
  • Fitting replacement
  • Upgrades
COM-05

Switchboard Assessment

Safety condition assessment of main switchboards and distribution boards, including thermal imaging.

  • Condition reports
  • Thermal imaging
  • Upgrade advice
  • Arc-risk review
COM-06

Safety Reports

Documented compliance records suitable for insurers, regulators, auditors and building owners.

  • Insurer reports
  • Regulatory records
  • Due diligence
  • Digital delivery
